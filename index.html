The opportunity is not to compete with Windows and macOS overnight — it is to build the most recognizable identity-based Linux brand for a rapidly growing audience.

CTP-EOS intersects the communities already exploring consciousness, creativity, technology and human potential — consciousness and wellness communities, spirituality and alternative spirituality, quantum and future-oriented communities, the UFO / UAP / disclosure communities, the Linux community, and the psytrance, EDM and creative communities. The UAP record demonstrates it: when a community moves from following information to participating in a movement, it needs something tangible — a computer platform, a product, a daily-use technology. That is what CTP-EOS provides.
